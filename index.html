3. Information stored locally

gamewe stores certain information locally on your device so that the App can operate and remember your progress and preferences. Depending on how you use the App, local information may include:

  • gameplay statistics and game scores;
  • personal bests and similar performance records;

4. Gameplay statistics and attempt data

gamewe currently provides free users with seven playable attempts per minigame per local calendar day. This limit is tracked separately for each minigame. When a particular minigame's free attempts are exhausted, the App may offer an optional rewarded advertisement. A successfully confirmed reward currently grants three additional attempts for that specific minigame.

Daily-attempt state, rewarded-attempt balances, and related game state are stored locally on the device. Unused rewarded bonus attempts currently reset with the next local calendar day.

gamewe offers Pro through auto-renewable subscriptions purchased through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple handles the payment transaction. gamewe does not receive your complete payment-card details.

Subscription management, renewal, and cancellation are handled through your Apple account and App Store subscription settings. Deleting gamewe does not automatically cancel an active Apple subscription. Refund requests and refund eligibility are handled under Apple's applicable processes and policies.

6. Rewarded advertisements

The current version of gamewe uses advertising only in the form of rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are not automatically displayed. A free user may voluntarily request one after exhausting the available attempts for a particular minigame.

Bonus attempts are granted only after the advertising provider confirms the reward. The current implementation does not use banner ads, interstitial ads, app-open ads, or automatic ad presentation.

Learn more from Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure guidance.
